# Scrolls of Light

A browser game for kids ages 8–15, inspired by Prodigy, set in the stories of the Bible.
Explore 40 Bible worlds, meet the people of the story, and battle Gloomlings (little shadows
of lies, worry, pride and fear) by answering questions. Every right answer powers your attack.

**Play:** once GitHub Pages is on, the game lives at `https://thias15.github.io/bible-prodigy/`.

## What's in the game

- **40 Bible worlds plus a hub town**, in Bible order and grouped into eras (Beginnings, Patriarchs,
  Exodus, Judges, Kings, Exile and Return, Jesus, the Early Church): Garden of Eden, Mountains of Ararat,
  Plains of Canaan (Abraham), Bethel (Jacob's ladder), Joseph's Journey, Mount Horeb (the burning bush), Land of Egypt, Wilderness of Sinai,
  Kadesh (the twelve spies), Jericho, Mount Tabor (Deborah), Oak of Ophrah (Gideon), Hills of Zorah (Samson), Fields of Ruth, Shiloh (Samuel), Valley of Elah, Solomon's Temple,
  Mount Carmel (Elijah), Dothan (Elisha), Road to Nineveh (Jonah), The Lost Scroll (Josiah), Babylon, the Lions' Den, Palace of Susa (Esther),
  Walls of Jerusalem (Nehemiah), Bethlehem (at night), The Temple Courts (Jesus at twelve), River Jordan (baptism and temptation), Cana,
  Sea of Galilee, Mount of Beatitudes, Road of Parables, Bethany (Lazarus), Jerusalem, Road to Emmaus, Pentecost, the Desert Road (Philip),
  Damascus Road (Saul), Paul's Voyage and the Isle of Patmos (a new heaven and a new earth) — connected by the Gates of Remembrance in Pilgrim's Rest.
- **A story to finish.** The Shadow of Doubt tore the Scroll of Light into forty pieces.
  Each world has a guide (Eve, Noah, Abraham, Joseph, Moses, Gideon, Ruth, David, Elijah,
  Jonah, Esther, Nehemiah, Peter, Paul...), a main quest, a boss, a helper quest and a memory verse.
  Every guide asks you a story choice ("Stay with Naomi or go home?") and tells you what
  really happened.
- **Prodigy-style battles.** Five powers (Faith Strike, Prayer, Hope Shield, Courage Blast,
  Wisdom Wave) and streak bonuses. Gloomlings show what they will do next (⚡ charging means
  a big hit is coming, so raise the Hope Shield). Every boss has its own mechanic from its
  story: build the ark plank by plank, bring the plagues, break the golden calf, march around
  Jericho seven times, sling a stone at Goliath with a streak, call down fire on Mount Carmel,
  calm the storm, rebuild Jerusalem's wall, find the lost sheep, coin and son, pray until
  Paul's chains fall off, and more.
- **Keeps going after the story:** daily and weekly challenges, champion rematches of every
  boss with rare rewards, animal friends that grow stronger (★ at level 4, ★★ at level 8,
  with special moves), and a Book of Discoveries to fill.
- **Mixed questions, no typing needed:** math (grade 2 to algebra) and Bible story math
  puzzles, Bible knowledge (about 250 questions with references), "Who said it?", "Where did
  this happen?" on a map of Bible lands, putting story events in order, memory verses, words
  (vocabulary and spelling) and puzzles (patterns, odd-one-out, books of the Bible).
- **Mini-games** at signposts in four worlds (also in the Scribe's list): gather manna,
  lead the animals into the ark, rebuild Nehemiah's wall, and fish on the Sea of Galilee.
- **Real learning, not just random questions.** Missed questions come back later (spaced
  repetition: a few minutes, then 1, 3, 7 and 21 days). Memory verses are learned in steps:
  fill one word, name the book, finish the verse, then name the reference. The game nudges
  difficulty per subject, and grown-ups get a weekly report in Settings.
- **Junior (8–10) or Explorer (11–15) play style:** three answer choices and gentler foes,
  or four choices, tougher bosses and a bonus for quick answers.
- **Accessibility:** large text, an easy-read font (Lexend), ✓/✗ marks on answers so color
  isn't needed, read-aloud, and reduced motion.
- **Progression:** levels, the Armor of God (Ephesians 6), animal companions that fight
  alongside you, robe dyes, a shop, practice mode and a verse library.
- **For grown-ups (⚙ Settings):** challenge level, subject mix sliders, read-aloud,
  per-subject scores, and a save code to move progress between devices.
- **English, German, Spanish and Greek.** Switch on the title screen or in Settings. Everything
  is translated: story, quests, all Bible questions, menus, mini-games and math word problems.
  Spelling questions use real spelling traps of each language (b/v and h in Spanish, η/ι/υ in Greek).
- **Original soundtrack** in a modern worship style, generated live in the browser: a theme
  for each world, a battle anthem and a boss theme, plus the public-domain melodies
  *Stille Nacht / Silent Night* (Bethlehem) and *Ode to Joy* ("Joyful, Joyful", the finale).
- **Read-aloud** for questions and dialogue, using the most natural voice on the device,
  with a voice picker and test button in Settings.
- **Animal teams.** One animal friend travels with you at first; a second joins at level 10 and a
  third at level 20. Pick your team and its leader in the Animals menu. Every animal acts in battle
  (bosses get a bit tougher for bigger teams), and ten Bible pairs give a team bonus, e.g. wolf and
  lamb ("Peace", Isaiah 11:6) heal you every turn, raven and dove ("Birds of the Ark") give more XP.
- **The Land opens step by step.** A road from Pilgrim's Rest leads into five connected regions that
  open along the journey, each after the stories that happen there: the Judean Desert after Jericho, the
  Jordan Valley after Shiloh, Around Jerusalem after Nineveh, the Coast after Nehemiah and the Galilee
  Hills after the Sea of Galilee. Each has a day/night
  cycle and its own weather (rain, mist, desert wind), a hidden cave with a treasure chest, a
  traveller with a new request every day (find lost things, answer questions, chase away
  gloomlings, share food), and rare wild animals that only live there (War Horse, Rock Badger at
  night, Dromedary...). After rain a rainbow may appear in Galilee. Twelve lost pages of Psalm 23
  are hidden in the regions, caves and islands.
- **Sea journeys with Paul** (after Paul's Voyage). From the Coast, sail the Great Sea in a little boat, steer around
  storm clouds and go ashore on Cyprus, Crete, Malta and in Rome. Each island has its story from
  Acts (Barnabas and the proconsul, Fair Havens, the shipwreck and the viper, Paul teaching in
  Rome), a quest, and a sea animal to befriend (dolphin, monk seal, crab, octopus, palace lizard).
- **Tower of the Watchman** (Habakkuk 2:1, opens after Jericho): an endless climb. Every floor is
  a battle, every 5th floor a boss, every 7th floor a day of rest (Genesis 2:2), foes get stronger as you go,
  your health carries over, and every 10 floors is a checkpoint with a trophy. Your highest floor shows on the friends leaderboard.
- **Story quests:** in nine earlier worlds someone from a smaller Bible story waits once you have met the guide: Rebekah at the well, Rahab and the scarlet cord, Gideon's fleece, Hannah's little robe for Samuel, David dancing before the ark, Anna in the temple, Peter's breakfast on the beach, the upper room, and Rhoda who forgot to open the door. Hear the story and answer one question for shekels and XP.
- **The New Quarter of Pilgrim's Rest** (opens after Shiloh): gather wood, stone, wool, fish and olive
  oil in the Land, on the islands and in the Tower, and build up a town: a well, bakery (daily bread),
  workshop (craft furniture for your house), animal pen (XP for animals not in your team), school
  (more shekels in practice), market, guest house (travellers you helped move in and bring gifts) and
  a lighthouse (fewer storms at sea). Each building has 3 levels; the town grows from Camp to Village,
  Town and City on a Hill. Friends see your town level and can send wood, stone and wool as gifts.
- **Variety:** seven hairstyles (including braids, afro and headscarf), three enemy types per
  world (quick, tough, healer), and about 50 animal friends with different abilities.
  The animal shop opens up as you go: cheap friends at the start, rarer ones after more
  finished worlds, and some (owl, bee, turtle...) only as world rewards.
- **Two ways to see the world.** A 3D view for exploring up close (characters, animals, landmarks
  and battles in 3D) and the 2D map view for a quick overview. Switch any time with the
  View button in the top bar or in Settings (battles can be 3D or 2D separately, and there is
  a "Low detail" option for older devices). Animal friends show as their familiar icons in
  both views; 3D animal models can be turned on in Settings. Each world has its own little
  scene (animals walking to the ark, shepherds and an angel at Bethlehem, fishing boats on
  Galilee, a crowd with palm branches in Jerusalem), and beating a boss plays a short scene. Without WebGL the game simply uses the 2D map.
- **Your own house** in Pilgrim's Rest: buy furniture from the house menu and place, turn or
  move it. Every finished world adds a treasure (Noah's ark model, David's harp, the manger...).
- **Wardrobe:** hair, skin and robe colors plus props to buy (headband, flower crown, olive
  wreath, shepherd staff, lantern, scroll, satchel, cape, bedroll). The Crown of Life is earned
  at the end of the journey.
- **Online (optional, off until a grown-up turns it on):** cloud saves that work on any device,
  friends added by friend code, a daily gift and stickers for each friend, a weekly friends
  leaderboard, groups with a shared weekly challenge (e.g. a Sunday-school class), visits to a
  friend's house, and live play: a quiz duel (same 10 questions) or a team boss battle. There is
  no chat and no typed text between players.
- Works with keyboard (arrows/WASD + Space), mouse (click to walk, wheel to zoom) and touch
  (drag anywhere for a joystick, tap to walk or talk, pinch to zoom).

## How saving works

- Progress saves automatically in the browser's local storage, which survives closing
  the tab or restarting the device. It is **not** synced between devices or browsers.
- Up to **4 players** per device, each with their own progress.
- The game asks the browser to keep its storage permanently.
- Settings → **Download backup file** saves a small `.json` file; **Restore from backup file**
  loads it on any device. A copyable save code works too. The game reminds players to
  back up after finishing a gate.
- Saves can be lost if someone clears browsing data or plays in a private window, so backups
  matter, or turn on Online.
- **Online saves** (Settings → Online, grown-up check): progress is saved on the device first and
  then online. Before a newer online save replaces the one on the device, the game keeps a copy
  (Settings → Online → earlier saves). If one player was played on two devices without internet,
  the game asks which save to keep and keeps the other as a backup.
- **Playing on another device:** each player gets a name like "Star Lamb 47" and can get a login
  card (name + 4 secret pictures), a continue code / QR code, or a grown-up can sign in with
  Google and bring the family's players to a new device ("I already play" on the title screen).
- Privacy: see [privacy.html](privacy.html). The owner dashboard is at `admin.html` (owner's Google
  account only).

## Hosting on GitHub Pages (free)

1. Push this repository (it already contains `index.html` at the root).
2. On GitHub open **Settings → Pages**.
3. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**, branch `main`, folder `/ (root)`, and save.
4. After a minute the game is live at `https://thias15.github.io/bible-prodigy/`.

No build step, server or accounts needed. On GitHub Pages the game can be installed like an app (Add to Home Screen) and keeps working offline. `three.min.js` (three.js r128, MIT license) sits next to `index.html` so the 3D view works without any outside server. Netlify, Cloudflare Pages or itch.io (upload `index.html`) also work.

## Editing the game

`index.html` is the playable build. The readable source is in `src/`:

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `src/data.js` | English content: chapters, dialogue, quests, Bible questions, verses, vocabulary, items |
| `src/i18n.js` | Interface text in both languages, plus the German content (merged over the English) |
| `src/worlds2.js` | Worlds 11–18 in both languages |
| `src/worlds3.js` | Worlds 19–22 (Gideon, Samson, Nehemiah, Parables) in both languages |
| `src/worlds_order.js` | `ORDER`, the gate sequence |
| `src/quiz_extra.js` | Order, who-said-it, map and story-math questions; story choices; map places |
| `src/companions.js` | Extra animal friends and the shop prices and unlock levels |
| `src/minigames.js` | The four mini-games |
| `src/game.js` | The engine: map generation, 2D drawing, movement, battles, question generators, menus |
| `src/r3d_lib.js` | 3D models (three.js): people and props, 29 animal friends, Gloomlings and bosses, landmarks, furniture |
| `src/r3d.js` | 3D view: world scenes, 3D battles, the house editor, thumbnails |
| `src/shell.html` | Page layout and styles |
| `src/lang/` | Spanish (`es/`) and Greek (`el/`) translations as JSON, plus `GLOSSARY.md` (names and terms) and the tools that check and pack them |
| `src/online_net.js` | Small data layer over Firebase (or the test server in `src/test/mockfb.js`) |
| `src/online.js` | Online accounts, cloud saves, login cards, continue codes, Settings → Online |
| `src/online_social.js` | Friends, gifts, stickers, leaderboards, groups, house visits |
| `src/online_live.js` | Quiz duel and team boss |
| `src/online_str.js` | English text for the online features (other languages in `src/lang/<l>/O.json`) |
| `src/ext_str.js` | The Land, sea, islands, Tower, wild animals and team text (other languages in `src/lang/<l>/X.json`) |
| `src/endgame.js` | The Land, the Great Sea, the islands and the Tower (inserted into `game.js` at `/*__EXT__*/`) |
| `src/worlds4.js` | Worlds 23–28 (Bethel, Shiloh, Solomon's Temple, River Jordan, Cana, Bethany) in English and German |
| `src/worlds6.js` | Worlds 35–40 (The Lost Scroll, Temple Courts, Road to Emmaus, Desert Road, Damascus Road, Isle of Patmos) in English and German |
| `src/quests.js` | Story quests inside earlier worlds (Rebekah, Rahab, Gideon's fleece, Hannah, David and the ark, Anna, Peter's breakfast, the Last Supper, Rhoda), all four languages (inserted after `town.js`) |
| `src/worlds5.js` | Worlds 29–34 (Mount Horeb, Kadesh, Mount Tabor, Dothan, the Lions' Den, Mount of Beatitudes) in English and German |
| `src/town.js` | The New Quarter town (inserted right after `endgame.js`) |
| `src/fb/` | Firebase config, security rules (`firestore.rules`) and the bundled SDK (`fb.js`, rebuilt with `npx esbuild entry.js --bundle --minify --format=iife --outfile=fb.js`) |
| `src/admin.template.html`, `src/privacy.html` | Owner dashboard and privacy page |
| `src/build.py` | Combines everything into `index.html`, `beta/index.html` (test copy with separate storage), `admin.html` and `privacy.html` (needs Python 3 and Node.js) |

To add a world, add it to `CH` (and `DE.CH`) in `src/worlds3.js` and put its id into `ORDER` in `src/worlds_order.js`
where it belongs in the story. Existing saves pick up new worlds automatically.

To add Bible questions, append rows to `BQ` in `src/data.js`:
`[chapter (0 = any), difficulty 1–3, question, correct answer, wrong, wrong, wrong, reference]`.
Add the German version at the same position in `BQ_DE` in `src/i18n.js`.
For Spanish and Greek, edit the JSON files in `src/lang/es/` and `src/lang/el/`; after changing English or German content, run
`node src/lang/make_src.js` and `node src/lang/check.js es A` (chunks A–D, languages es/el) to find what needs translating.
Then run `python3 src/build.py` and copy `src/dist/index.html` and `src/dist/sw.js` to the repo root (the icons, `manifest.json`, `three.min.js` and `models/` only change rarely).

## Bible text

- **English:** World English Bible (public domain), with "Yahweh" shown as "the LORD".
- **German:** short, kid-friendly retellings written for this game and labeled "nach …"
  (e.g. "nach Johannes 3,16"). They are not an official translation. Translations such as
  *Hoffnung für alle* are copyrighted and can't be republished here, so families can look up
  each reference in their own Bible.
- **Spanish and Greek:** kid-friendly retellings written for this game, shown as "según Juan 3:16"
  and "Ιωάννης 3:16 (απόδοση)". Modern translations such as RVR1960, NVI or the Greek Bible Society's
  version are copyrighted, so families can look up each reference in their own Bible.
- Music contains no copyrighted songs.
